# Sedenion Zero-Divisor Sheaf & Exceptional Albert-Jordan Transceiver (SZD-AJT)

Discrete cellular sheaf cohomology over the 16D Cayley-Dickson sedenion zero-divisor variety and the 27D Exceptional Albert Jordan algebra.

## Key Foundations

1. **168 Sedenion Zero-Divisors**:
   Discovers and maps the 168 discrete orthogonal zero-divisor pairs $(e_i \pm e_j) \cdot (e_k \pm e_l) \equiv 0$ in $\mathbb{S}^{16}$.

2. **27D Albert-Freudenthal Cubic Invariant**:
   Implements the cubic determinant $\det(X) = r_1 r_2 r_3 - \sum r_i |x_j|^2 + 2\operatorname{Re}(x \cdot z \cdot y)$ invariant under $F_4$.

3. **Singular Sheaf Nullspace**:
   Sheaf Laplacian $L_{\text{ZD}} \in M_{64}(\mathbb{R})$ over 4-node tetrahedron isolates 4 protected harmonic channels via zero-divisor annihilation projection.
