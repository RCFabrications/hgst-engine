# 2-Category Sheaf & Beilinson Monad Transceiver (2CAT-SMT)

Discrete cellular sheaves elevated to 2-categories with Higher Hodge-Helmholtz 1-Laplacians and Beilinson instanton quiver monads.

## Key Invariants

1. **Beilinson Instanton Monad**:
   Linear sequence $\mathbb{C}^k \xrightarrow{\alpha} \mathbb{C}^{r+2k} \xrightarrow{\beta} \mathbb{C}^k$ with $\beta \circ \alpha = 0$ isolating physical gauge rank $r = \dim(\ker \beta / \operatorname{im} \alpha)$.

2. **Higher Hodge 1-Laplacian**:
   $L_1 = \delta_0 \delta_0^\top + \delta_1^\top \delta_1 \in M_{12}(\mathbb{R})$ over $K_4$ tetrahedron enforcing coboundary nilpotency $\delta_1 \circ \delta_0 \equiv 0$.
