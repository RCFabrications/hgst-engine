   Decomposes into two scalars $(\alpha, \beta)$ and two $3 \times 3$ octonionic matrices $(X, Y) \in \mathcal{J}_3(\mathbb{O})$.

2. **Symplectic Invariance**:
   Transport maps preserve the skew-symmetric form $\Omega \in \operatorname{Sp}(56, \mathbb{R})$ ($g^\top \Omega g = \Omega$).

3. **8D Harmonic Entanglement Nullspace**:
   Sheaf Laplacian $L_{E_7} \in M_{224}(\mathbb{R})$ over 4-node tetrahedral network isolates 8 protected harmonic channels.
