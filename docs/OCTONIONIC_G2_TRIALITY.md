# The Octonionic G2-Triality Quantum Stabilizer Transceiver (O-G2T)

Discrete non-associative cellular sheaf cohomology over the self-dual 24-cell (Octacube) 4-polytope.
Utilizes the exceptional Lie group $G_2 = \operatorname{Aut}(\mathbb{O})$ to annihilate the associator tensor $[x, y, z] = 0$, restoring exact 2-step simplicial boundary nilpotency $\partial_1 \partial_2 \equiv 0 \pmod 2$.
