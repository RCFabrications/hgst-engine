# E8 Exceptional Heterotic Root Lattice Sheaf Transceiver (E8-HT)

Discrete cellular sheaf cohomology over the unique 8-dimensional even unimodular lattice $E_8$ (Gosset $4_{21}$ polytope).

## Key Foundations

1. **240 Roots of Norm 2**:
   112 roots of $D_8$ plus 128 half-integer spinor weight vectors with even parity.
   Cartan determinant $\det(A_{E_8}) = 1$ enforces unimodular gauge quantization.

2. **Weyl Sheaf Nullspace**:
   Sheaf Laplacian $L_{E_8} \in M_{64}(\mathbb{R})$ over 8-node loop with Weyl reflection $s_\alpha \in W(E_8)$ preserves exactly 7 unbroken gauge current modes ($\dim \ker L_{E_8} = 7$).
